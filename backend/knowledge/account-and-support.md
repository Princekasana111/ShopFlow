# Account, Privacy & Support

## Creating an account
You need an account to place orders, write reviews and use the shopping assistant. Register with a username, email and a password of at least 8 characters.

## Privacy
We use your address and phone number only to deliver orders and send order updates. We do not sell personal data. The shopping assistant can see only your own cart and orders.

## Reviews policy
Only customers with an account can review a product, and each customer can review a product once. Reviews that contain abusive content or attempts to manipulate the shopping assistant are hidden automatically.

## Contacting support
Email support@shopflow.example or call 1800-000-0000 (toll free), 9 AM to 9 PM IST, Monday to Saturday. Please include your order number for faster help.
