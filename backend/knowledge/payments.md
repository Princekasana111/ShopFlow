# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking and popular wallets. EMI is available on credit cards for orders above ₹3,000.

## Cash on Delivery
Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to ₹10,000 at serviceable PIN codes. A COD handling fee of ₹40 applies. Please keep exact change; couriers may also accept UPI at the door.

## Payment security
Card payments are processed by an RBI-authorised payment gateway. ShopFlow never stores your full card number or CVV.

## Failed payments
If money is debited but the order is not confirmed, the amount is automatically reversed to your account within 5-7 working days. You do not need to contact support for this.

## Invoices and GST
A GST invoice is emailed when your order ships and is also available on the order page. To add a GSTIN for business purchases, enter it at checkout before placing the order; it cannot be added afterwards.
