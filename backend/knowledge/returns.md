# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
Most products can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Electronics have a 7-day replacement-only window: a defective or damaged electronic item is replaced with the same model, not refunded, unless the replacement is out of stock.

## Items that cannot be returned
Innerwear, socks, opened personal-care items, books with writing or damage caused after delivery, and products marked "Non-returnable" on the product page cannot be returned. Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, unwashed and returned in the original packaging with all tags, accessories, manuals and freebies. Footwear must be returned with the original shoe box. Returns that fail the quality check are sent back to the customer.

## How to start a return
Open My Orders, choose the delivered order and select Return. Pickup is arranged within 2 working days at no cost. If pickup is not available at your PIN code, you can self-ship the item and we reimburse up to ₹100 of courier charges.

## Refund timelines
Refunds are issued after the returned item passes the quality check at our warehouse. Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. Cash on Delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer (NEFT) within 7 working days after you share your bank details.

## Cancelling an order
A pending order can be cancelled from My Orders before it ships, and the full amount is refunded. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled; refuse the delivery or start a return after it arrives.
