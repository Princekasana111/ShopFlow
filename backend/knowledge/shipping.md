# Shipping & Delivery

## Delivery areas
ShopFlow delivers to over 19,000 PIN codes across India. Enter your PIN code at checkout to confirm delivery to your address. We do not ship outside India.

## Delivery timelines
Orders placed before 2 PM on a working day are dispatched the same day. Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) usually receive orders in 2-4 working days. Other locations take 4-7 working days. North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands can take up to 10 working days.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more. Orders below ₹999 have a flat delivery charge of ₹79. Express delivery (next working day, metro cities only) costs ₹149 and must be selected before payment.

## Order tracking
Every order has a status page under My Orders. The status moves from Pending (order received) to Shipped (handed to the courier) to Delivered. You receive an SMS with the courier tracking number once the order ships.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier makes up to three delivery attempts. If all three fail, the package is returned to our warehouse and the full amount is refunded within 7 working days of the package reaching us, minus any shipping charge.
