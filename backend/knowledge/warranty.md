# Warranty

## Manufacturer warranty
Electronics and appliances carry the manufacturer's warranty stated on the product page, usually 1 year from the date of delivery. Keep your ShopFlow invoice: it is required for any warranty claim.

## How to claim warranty
Contact the brand's authorised service centre listed on the warranty card, or write to ShopFlow support with your order number and we will share the service centre details. ShopFlow handles replacements only within the 7-day replacement window; after that, the brand handles repairs.

## What warranty does not cover
Physical damage, liquid damage, damage from using third-party chargers, normal wear and tear, and products repaired by unauthorised technicians are not covered.
